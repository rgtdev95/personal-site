The contact form

When you send a message, I receive your name, email address, business name (if given) and message by email. I use them only to reply and to discuss working together, and I don't sell or share them. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Booking a call

Booking uses Calendly, a separate service with its own privacy policy. The details you enter there are shared with me so I can hold the call.

Visitor statistics

This site uses Umami, self-hosted on my own server, to count visits and see which pages are useful. It sets no cookies, doesn't track you across other sites, and doesn't store personal information.

Cookies

This site sets one cookie, and only on pages with the contact form. It's called session and holds a random security token that stops other websites from sending the form in your name. It contains nothing about you, isn't used for tracking or advertising, and is deleted when you close your browser.

Because it's needed for the form to work safely, it doesn't need your consent, so there's no cookie banner. There are no analytics, advertising or social media cookies.

Videos

Videos on this site are YouTube embeds in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube doesn't set cookies when the page loads, but once you press play, YouTube (Google) may store information on your device under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email {{ contact_email }}.
