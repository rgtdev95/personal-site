# Surface brief: Home (and the site's shared world)

Scope: homepage of ronsystemsai.com, plus the shared world every page inherits (base layout, nav, footer, docket components). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: owners/ops managers of small and medium businesses in English-speaking markets, non-technical, wary of jargon, upsell and lock-in. Job: understand what Ron fixes and trust him enough to book. Action: Book a free 20-minute call (Calendly), email as the alternative. Proof: 11+ years in IT, 4 years 24/7 financial production support, three anonymized case studies, vendor-neutral, client owns everything. No prices, no time-zone talk, no client-location claims, no testimonials (none exist).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Work that moves itself. Every manual task in a business is a printed docket; Ron turns a pile of handwritten tickets into dockets that flow along one rail to "done". Refuses the category-default consultancy page (hero + tool-hub diagram + icon cards on blue-accent white).

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper ground (#F3F4F1), white thermal dockets with torn/serrated bottom edges and faint print texture, brushed-stainless rail (#B8BEC5 gradients, rivets), print black #17171B, one printer red #D62839 used only for the "changed/automated" state, strikes, and the primary action. Dockets obey printer discipline: two sizes, rank by weight, caps, red ink and reversed black bars. One 42-column docket module sets widths. Condensed heavy display (Big Shoulders Display), readable grotesk body (Hanken Grotesk), monospace (Martian Mono) only for docket data.

STORY: The visitor sees their own chaos as dockets (re-typing invoices, chasing leads, copying spreadsheets), watches one get automated, understands the offer in plain English, sees how the work runs (8 numbered stages on the rail), sees real past builds as filed dockets, and tears off the stub to book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width steel rail across the top under a slim nav. Left 6/12: headline "Stop running your business on manual work." at ~5.5rem condensed caps-free, one plain paragraph, primary red tear-off stub "Book a free call" plus a quiet text link "See how it works". Right 6/12: three example dockets (labeled "Example") hanging from the rail; the front one prints line by line, its tasks get struck in red with "AUTOMATED", then it slides right along the rail to a done spike while the stack underneath stays visible.

FORM: The Docket Rail, position 7 of my ordered list (assigned by the roll), seed key 28a52611. Raises kept: printer type discipline (Timetable), real ticket IDs as addresses with sequence only where it is real (Teletext, reconciled with the craft floor), 42-column module (ASCII), manual layer stays visible (Torn Flyer), one decisive tangle-to-flow moment (Accretion). Signature interaction: the hero docket print-and-slide, orchestrated once, with reduced motion showing the finished state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
