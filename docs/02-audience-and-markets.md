# 02 — Audience and markets

## Who the site is for

### Primary: small and medium business owners and operators

- Roughly 2–100 staff. The owner, a director, or an operations/office manager who has become "the IT person" by accident.
- **Situation:** the business grew on a pile of apps (spreadsheets, email, a CRM, accounting, a booking tool, chat). The tools don't talk to each other, staff re-type the same data, and one person knows how everything works.
- **What they want:** fewer hours lost to admin, fewer mistakes, clarity on what to buy, and someone trustworthy to set it up properly.
- **What they fear:** being upsold, being locked in, jargon, an expensive project that goes nowhere, and AI hype.
- **Typical sectors (to validate over time):** professional services (accounting, legal, consulting), clinics and wellness, trades and field services, e-commerce and retail, agencies, education/coaching, real estate.

### Later (skipped for now): individuals who need "a tech guy"

> Decision 2026-09-30: the site targets SMBs only at launch. Notes kept for later.

- Solo professionals, founders, creators, and consultants running their work on their own.
- **Needs:** set up and automate their tools, a personal AI assistant or agent, a website/booking/payment flow, home-office or self-hosted setup, or "just make this work."
- **Watch-out:** smaller budgets. If added later, give them a smaller fixed-scope option so they don't squeeze out the SMB work.

### Not the audience (for now)

- Enterprises running procurement processes. The background impresses them, but the sales cycle doesn't fit freelancing.
- Recruiters. They're served by LinkedIn, not this site.

### Decisions this creates

- **Language:** English with US spelling site-wide (decided). Don't mix spellings.
- **Pricing:** not shown on the site (decided). Projects are scoped and quoted after the discovery call.
- **Time zones:** not addressed on the site (decided). No time-zone FAQ, no working-hours or response-time promises. Ron manages call availability in Calendly, which shows each visitor slots in their local time.
- **Location pages:** add one market page per region only when it has real, different content (see [05-search-and-ai-visibility.md](05-search-and-ai-visibility.md)).

> Decided: the site copy makes no client-location claims (no "Working with clients in…" / "Available to clients in…"). The initial design's line is dropped. Target markets live only in structured data (`areaServed`).
