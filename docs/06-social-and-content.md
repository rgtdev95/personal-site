## One piece of work → many outputs

```
1 topic (e.g. "Automate new-lead follow-up with n8n")
 ├── YouTube video (8–15 min walkthrough)
 ├── Website article (/resources/...) with the video embedded + transcript + steps
 ├── 2–3 YouTube Shorts / Facebook Reels (60 s clips)
 ├── Facebook post (+ share in relevant groups where allowed)
 └── LinkedIn post (lesson learned, business angle)
```

## YouTube channel plan

- **Name:** Ron Systems AI (`@ronsystemsai`).
- **Content pillars:**
  1. _Automate it_: real workflow builds (n8n / Zapier / Make) for common SMB tasks
  2. _AI that works_: practical AI assistants and agents, honest reviews, what's hype
  3. _Tool decisions_: X vs Y comparisons, "is this worth paying for?"
  4. _Behind the build_: anonymized client case studies, lessons from the 24/7 support years
- **Every video:** a clear title phrased as a question or outcome, a description whose first two lines link to the matching site page, chapters, accurate captions (edit the auto-captions), and a pinned comment linking to the free call.
- **Cadence:** pick what you can sustain (e.g. 2 videos per month + Shorts cut from them). Consistency beats volume.
