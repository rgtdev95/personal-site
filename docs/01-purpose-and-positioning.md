# 01 — Purpose and positioning

**Brand:** Ron Systems AI · **Domain:** ronsystemsai.com · **Person behind it:** Rolando "Ron" Tactay

## Why this website exists

To win **freelance, project-based work** from small and medium businesses, without the visitor having to understand the technology first.

The site succeeds when a busy business owner who has never met Rolando:

1. recognises their own problem in the first screen,
2. understands in plain words what Rolando would do about it,
3. trusts that he can actually do it, and
4. books a free call (or sends a message).

A second job: be **the source that search engines and AI assistants quote** when someone asks "who can help my small business automate X" or "should I use Zapier or n8n". See [05-search-and-ai-visibility.md](05-search-and-ai-visibility.md).

## One-line positioning

> An experienced IT engineer who looks at how your business runs, tells you honestly what to fix, and then builds it: automations, AI assistants, and systems that keep working after he leaves.

Short versions for bios and profiles:

- **12 words:** I fix the tech behind small businesses: automation, AI, and systems that work.
- **Tagline candidates:** "Stop running your business on manual work." (current hero) · "Your tech, sorted." · "Less busywork. Systems that run themselves."

## What the website is not

- Not a job-seeking CV site. The resume is source material and doesn't get published as-is. (If a "hire me full-time" audience matters later, add an `/about` link to LinkedIn and leave it at that.)
- Not a SaaS product or an agency pretending to be bigger than one person. Being a single experienced person is a strength: direct contact, one accountable person.
- Not a tools showcase. Tool names (n8n, Zapier, Hermes, OpenClaw…) support credibility and search, but the headlines are always about business outcomes.
